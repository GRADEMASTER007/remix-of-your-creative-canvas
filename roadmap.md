# Roadmap — Living Culture Health marketplace

African heritage marketplace: foods, baking, traditional medicinals, African art.

1. [in progress] Enable Lovable Cloud (database + accounts)
2. Design system — earth-tone African heritage theme (terracotta/ochre, warm serif display)
3. Database: profiles (seller stores), products, orders, plan tiers
4. Accounts: email/password + Google sign-in, sign-in page
5. Public marketplace: home, browse by category, product detail, seller storefront
6. Seller dashboard: upload/edit products, store profile; limits 20 products (Starter) / 100 products (Heritage)
7. Pricing page for the two tiers
8. Checkout: PayPal + PayFast gateways, order records, payment notifications
9. Social media sharing on product pages (WhatsApp, Facebook, X, copy link)
10. Demo products seeded so the marketplace looks alive
11. Ask user for PayPal + PayFast credentials (secure secret form) before live payments
